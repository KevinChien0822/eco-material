Environmental Issues

Our Environmental Commitment

Understanding plastic hazards and making better choices for the planet

🌊

The Severity of Plastic Pollution

Around 78 million tonnes of plastic packaging are produced globally each year, and about 32% is released into the environment. An estimated 25 million tonnes end up in the ocean. Conventional plastics take centuries to decompose.

🌱

Advantages of Biodegradable Materials

Under proper composting conditions, biodegradable materials fully decompose and return to nature without releasing plastic microparticles. Made from natural plant fibers, they support a circular economy.

⚠️

Biodegradable Claims: Know the Difference

Many products labeled 'biodegradable' are actually conventional plastics mixed with other substances that only break down into microparticles and fail international compostability certification. Mao Mao Feng Lin's materials are genuinely biodegradable/compostable resins.

🌍

Circular Economy and the Future

Global bio-resin production capacity continues to grow, reflecting rising demand for eco-friendly materials. Choosing biodegradable materials is a commitment to a better environment for the next generation.

Genuinely Biodegradable vs. False Claims

Many products labeled 'biodegradable' do not actually decompose

✕ False Biodegradable

  • ✕ Conventional plastic + photosensitizers → breaks into fragments
  • ✕ Conventional plastic + corn starch/calcium carbonate → breaks into microparticles
  • ✕ Conventional plastic + oxo additives → breaks into powder
  • ✕ Fails international compostability certification
  • ✕ Still releases plastic microparticles into the environment
  • ✕ Oxo-degradable plastics are banned in the EU

✓ Genuinely Biodegradable

  • ✓ Made from 100% plant fiber composite materials
  • ✓ Complies with international compostability standard (EN 13432)
  • ✓ Fully decomposes and returns to nature
  • ✓ Does not release plastic microparticles
  • ✓ Passes SGS / Intertek testing
  • ✓ Verified by Beta bio-based content testing
